### Shared implementation priorities

This retained shared planning snapshot includes steps with later landings. Check the current

- Extend analytic cross-checks beyond Beer-Lambert (the framework is data-driven via `AnalyticCheckResult.measuredField`). **[charged-particle CSDA range landed 2026-06-30]** `analytic_csda_range.js` derives the CSDA range from Geant4's own stopping power (`G4EmCalculator::GetCSDARange`) and compares it to a new `primary_mean_track_length_mm` tally — 20 MeV proton in water, derived 4.282 mm vs measured 4.266 mm (0.38%); guarded by `analytic_csda_range_cross_check`. **[photofraction landed 2026-07-02]** `analytic_photo_fraction.js` predicts the photoelectric process-branching ratio `sigma_phot/sigma_total` from the SAME per-process cross sections Beer-Lambert sums and compares it to a new `primaries_photoelectric_first_fraction` tally (the fraction of primaries whose first discrete interaction is photoelectric, classified through QBBC's `G4GammaGeneralProcess` wrapper by EM subtype) — 30 keV gamma in water, derived 0.3908 vs measured 0.3931 (0.59%); guarded by `analytic_photo_fraction_cross_check`. Unlike the two attenuation/range checks this one tests the process CHOICE (branching), and is slab-thickness independent. **[Klein-Nishina Compton transfer + photofraction energy sweep landed 2026-11-06]** `analytic_compton_transfer.js` compares the free-electron KN mean energy-transfer fraction `σ_tr/σ` with Geant4's sampled first-Compton `(E−E')/E` (662 keV in water: 0.3816 vs 0.3825, 0.26%, z=+0.56) and bounds the measured maximum by the Compton edge; `analytic_photo_fraction_energy_sweep` reruns the photofraction at 10–100 keV via `--param energy_mev` (all |z| < 1, 57× span). **[binned KN spectrum + pair-production threshold/branching landed 2026-11-07]** the Compton check also grades the 20-bin `u = T/T_max` spectrum shape (χ² = 10.5/19) and `analytic_pair_production.js` compares `σ_conv/σ_total` with the measured first-conversion fraction (10 MeV: 0.2339 vs 0.2365, z = +0.83; exactly zero below 1.022 MeV). **[KN angular distribution landed 2026-11-07]** the Compton check also grades the scattered photon's direction (`<cos θ>` z = −0.41, 20-bin `cos θ` χ² = 12.6/19) and the free-electron energy-angle closure (max residual 3.3e-16). **[photonuclear-aware totals + giant-dipole check landed 2026-11-07]** photon `mu_total` now includes Geant4's gamma inelastic cross section; the pair sweep reaches 30 MeV and a `photonuclear_fraction` check peaks at 23 MeV (0.0491 derived vs 0.0501 measured, z = +0.55). **[photonuclear final state landed 2026-11-07]** four-momentum closure (≤ 2.2e-12), target-isotope identity and the two-body ejectile edge pass at 15/23/30 MeV (`analytic_photonuclear_final_state`); the reported yields show Geant4 11.4's `GammaNPreco` sends 16O mostly to α break-up (0.006 n per absorption at 23 MeV), a recorded channel-mix gap. Details and next checks (bound-electron Compton, independent XCOM/IAEA, photonuclear channel mix) live in the [simulation roadmap](../engine/simulation/ROADMAP.md).

## Refresh recorded media

[`scripts/update_experiment_media.sh`](../../scripts/update_experiment_media.sh)
runs the classic and Studio capture workflows and promotes successful captures to their
committed collections. Use `--list` to see coverage and `--resume <session-dir>` to
reuse matching work under `build/media_refresh/`. The per-session audit reports blocked
runs and static-only scenes. Native screenshots have their own capture guides.
